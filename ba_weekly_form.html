Use this checklist every Thursday before submitting your update. Check off each item to confirm it has been completed to the expected standard — not just filled in, but answered with the quality the TPM needs to report up. Check items off as you go; they'll stay checked until you reset.

A
BA & Sprint Context
Required
  • BA name entered — full name, not initials
  • Week ending date selected
  • Sprint number entered
    e.g. "Sprint 42" — not just a number
  • Sprint dates entered
    e.g. "Apr 21 – May 2"
  • At least one engineering team checked
    Dynamics/DOX/Tech Enablement · Data Engineering · Reporting & Analytics · AppDev · Other
  • Primary initiative(s) named — not just a team name
    Include the initiative name and epic ID if available
B
Sprint Run-Down
Required
  • Story points committed entered — zero is valid, blank is not
  • Story points completed entered — zero is valid, blank is not
  • Carryover yes/no answered
    If Yes: what carried over is named, reason category selected, and context explains why it happened
  • Happy carryover yes/no answered
    If Yes: what moved and why it is a positive outcome are both explained specifically
C
Prior Sprint Outcomes
Required
  • Key work delivered — 1 to 3 bullets, deliverables not activities
    Bad: "worked on AC" · Good: "Finalized and approved AC for COC-289 — unblocks build in Sprint 43"
  • Biggest win is outcome-focused, not task-focused
    Describe the impact and what it enables, not just what was completed
  • Improvement focus is specific — names the behavior, process, or gap
    Bad: "estimating" · Good: "Story sizing — three tickets were undersized; recommend pre-sprint sizing review with dev lead"
D
Weekly Execution Breakdown
Required
  • 3 to 5 meaningful work items listed — not a task list
    Focus on items with decisions, dependencies, or forward momentum
  • Each item has a status selected
  • Each item is linked to an epic, initiative, or issue
  • Each item has a next step — even Completed items
  • Blocked items include: what is blocking, who owns it, and delivery impact
    A blocked item with no owner or impact stated is not actionable
E
SNOW / Incident Signal
Required
  • SNOW tickets opened entered — zero is valid, blank is not
  • SNOW tickets closed entered — zero is valid, blank is not
  • P1 / escalation yes/no answered
    If Yes: all three fields completed — what happened, current status, and next steps with owners
F
Health, Dependencies & Escalation
Required
  • At least one initiative rated in the health table
    Use the definitions — health reflects trajectory, not current optimism
  • Green initiatives: planned next week is stated (or "no updates")
  • Amber initiatives: all three fields complete — what is at risk, path to green with owner, and next week plan
  • Red initiatives: what is blocked with delivery impact, and unblock owner and deadline stated
    Red = do not wait for Thursday — alert TPM immediately
  • Dependencies, risks, or blockers field completed — "None at this time" is acceptable if accurate
    Name the owner and resolution path for anything active
  • TPM help type selected — at least one button active
  • TPM detail field completed with specifics of what is needed and by when
    "None at this time" is acceptable if you selected that button
⚠ When to send an ad hoc update — do not wait for Thursday
A blocker puts a committed release date or sprint commitment at immediate risk
A P1 or escalation-risk incident is opened or remains unresolved
UAT cannot be completed before a planned release window
A cross-team dependency is unresolved and time-sensitive
An issue is likely to surface in a leadership or steering committee meeting
A scope change affects multiple teams or more than one sprint
A key stakeholder has been or is about to be affected and has not been informed
When in doubt — escalate. Over-communication is always safer than silence.
